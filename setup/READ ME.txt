Teams Calendar for iCUE - Setup
===============================

1. Double-click  Setup.cmd
   It installs what's needed, asks for your Outlook calendar link,
   and copies your widget access key to the clipboard.

2. In iCUE, paste the key into the Teams Calendar widget's
   "Helper Access Key" setting.

That's it. To remove everything later, double-click Uninstall.cmd.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and
then "Run anyway" (the scripts are plain text - open them in Notepad to
see exactly what they do).

Full guide: https://github.com/font-max/teams-calendar-icue#quick-setup
